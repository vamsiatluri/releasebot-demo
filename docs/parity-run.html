    What this actually proves

    The copy was built from the original, not from its paperwork

    The starting point was a reading of the running account, not of the documents describing it. On a system live for years, the two are never quite the same — somebody changed something during an incident and never wrote it down.

    Three attempts failed before anything was created

    That is the loop earning its keep. Each failure named a real problem — permissions that are account-wide rather than regional, labels the platform reserves for itself, a setting that has to exist once per region before anything else works.

    The original never went down

    The source account stayed live and serving for the whole exercise. That is what makes this a rehearsal rather than a leap: until somebody chooses to switch over, the thing that works is still the thing that works.

    “Finished” is a number you can read

    Not an assurance in a meeting. Eight differences, zero blocking, each with a named reason — a line a reviewer can sign rather than a claim they have to take on trust.
